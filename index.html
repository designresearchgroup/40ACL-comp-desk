A living record. Grants, reporting rules and treatment logs are editable and saved on this device (localStorage) in this draft — the production build syncs to the League’s secure record (multi-device), sends the email reminders, auto-drafts the reviewer-ready reports from award language, and layers in the digital-twin spatial data (PG&E, water, CalFIRE, parcels, snowpack) and the forestsat.space monitoring pilot. Grant $ amounts and acres are real (Phase I proposal); reporting cadences & acre targets are placeholders until the grant files are loaded — edit any grant to set them.
