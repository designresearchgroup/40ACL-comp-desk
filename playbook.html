Where the League stands

A land trust ready for its second inning

The acquisition engine works — 700+ acres conserved, 500+ in pursuit, $10M+ secured. But 80%+ of that capital is restricted to acquisition, and every new acre adds linear staff burden because the infrastructure to run it hasn't caught up to the ambition. Three things stand between here and 20,000 acres:

  • ● Critical Post-award compliance drift. Six concurrent grants, four monitored directly by state agencies, tracked in disconnected spreadsheets — reporting deadlines and tax-credit documentation easy to miss as the portfolio grows.
  • Spatial data fragmentation. PG&E, water districts, CAL FIRE fuels, parcels and snowpack live in separate portals. Every stewardship decision means manual cross-referencing; the digital twin doesn't exist yet.
  • Stewardship funding gap. ~$1M for long-term restoration against $10M+ for acquisition. The 20K-acre goal needs multi-year stewardship capital, impact debt and aligned philanthropy — which funders give to what they can see.
The proof · already delivered

The operating model

Four systems, one partner running them

HumanData facilitates the operations the League's mission depends on but shouldn't have to staff. The compliance desk is the first; the other three build on the same record.

01 · Compliance & reporting

The portfolio runs itself live

The desk tracks every obligation, reminds ahead of every deadline, and drafts reviewer-ready reports from the record. No report slips; no scramble.

02 · Data infrastructure

From one record to a digital twin next

The shared record today; the digital twin next — PG&E, water, CAL FIRE fuels, parcels and snowpack layered into one working map, so a stewardship decision is one query, not a day of cross-referencing.

03 · Grants & capital

Find, draft, win — and diversify next

Sourcing, drafting and compliance across the multi-funder portfolio, then diversification into family offices, impact debt and the federal pipeline (NRCS, USFS, IRA). Clean compliance makes the next proposal twice as fast and twice as credible.

04 · Narrative & positioning

The story catches up to the work next

Impact reporting and the funder-facing assets — capability statement, 30x30 co-authorship, the pitch that puts Jade in the rooms where the next $50M is allocated. Funders fund what they can see.
