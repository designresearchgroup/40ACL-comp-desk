● Critical blocker · Phase I

Post-award compliance drift

As the portfolio grows, reporting deadlines, tax-credit documentation and treatment-dashboard updates get harder to hold. One missed report is a crack in the record the next funder reads. The acquisition wins are visible; the stewardship work that proves them is not — yet.

  • Six concurrent grants — four monitored directly by state agencies (WCB, SNC) — tracked across disconnected spreadsheets and inboxes.
  • Reporting cadence varies by funder and consumes scarce staff time, scramble by scramble.
  • 80%+ of secured capital is acquisition; ~$1M funds long-term stewardship. The 20K-acre goal can't be met on acquisition grants alone.
  • Reactive reporting where funders reward proactive, on-time impact — the storytelling layer has to catch up to the fieldwork.
What we built

One system, three moving parts

Not a prettier spreadsheet — a compliance engine with a shared record underneath it and notifications on top. Here is the whole of it.

01 · The app

A living compliance engine

Every grant carries its status — on-track, due-soon, overdue, data-stale — and the desk ranks what needs attention now. Filing a report resets the clock automatically.

02 · The database

One shared record

Grants, acres, vendors and logs sync across every device through a secure record. It works offline in the forest and reconciles when back in coverage. Drop in a CSV and it maps itself.

03 · Notifications

Nothing slips

Automatic email reminders before every deadline, and reviewer-ready progress reports drafted straight from the record — so a report is never written from scratch, or late.

The app

It tells you what to do next

Open the desk and the first thing you see is the queue — ranked by what's most at risk. Each grant shows its reporting status and its funder-set target. The cadences are pre-filled from each funder's published guidelines and clearly marked [confirm] until you verify them against the signed agreement — honest by default, never a guessed deadline passed off as fact.

The database

The record that compounds

Every acre treated, every vendor invoice, every filed report lands in one secure record — not a folder of spreadsheets that only one person can reconcile. That record is what turns compliance into capital: a continuous evidence base that makes the next proposal twice as fast to write and twice as credible to read.

  • Syncs across every device — office, phone, field tablet — from one source of truth.
  • Offline field capture — log treatment with no signal; it saves on-device and syncs when back in coverage.
  • CSV in, organized out — drop a grant list or treatment log and the desk detects what it is and maps the columns.
Notifications

A report never slips through

The desk watches every deadline so your team doesn't have to. A daily job emails one clean digest of what's coming due — 60, 30, 14, 7 days out and overdue — straight to the people responsible. Report ahead of the funding cycle, not at the deadline.
