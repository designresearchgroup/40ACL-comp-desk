Overview

The 40 Acre Conservation League is a 501(c)(3) land trust and the first and only Black-led conservation land trust in California. We acquire and steward working forestland, restore watersheds and wildfire-resilient landscapes, and build equitable access to the outdoors — pairing biodiversity with human diversity. Founded by Jade Stevens, the League has conserved 700+ acres with 500+ in active pursuit, toward a 20,000-acre coalition goal by 2030.

Core Competencies

  • Land acquisition & conservation — fee and easement, working forestland
  • Forest health & fuels reduction — wildfire-resilience treatment
  • Watershed & hydrology restoration
  • Recreation planning & equitable access
  • On-site timber milling — value-added stewardship
  • Community engagement — inclusive-conservation convening
  • Grant management & compliance — multi-funder portfolio
  • Impact reporting & 30x30 alignment

Past Performance

Tahoe Forest Gateway — acquisition of the historic Leidesdorff property (named for William Leidesdorff, California's first Black millionaire). Blue Canyon Bar — 26-acre Tahoe National Forest rest stop integrated into the portfolio. 404.98 acres treated and 200,000 board feet milled on-site for the Nature Center in 2025. $10M+ secured for acquisition and $1M+ deployed to restoration, recreation, and hydrology across six concurrent grants. Convened Black Conservation Week at the State Capitol and featured in California's 30x30 progress reports as a model for inclusive conservation.

Differentiators

  • California's first & only Black-led conservation land trust — a trusted, distinctive voice in state and federal conservation.
  • Reclaiming land tied to William Leidesdorff — conservation and historical justice as one mission.
  • Active partnerships with the state agencies that steward California's lands (WCB, SNC, CAL FIRE) and federal land managers (USFS).
  • Data-driven grant compliance and continuous impact-reporting infrastructure — reviewer-ready evidence on demand.
